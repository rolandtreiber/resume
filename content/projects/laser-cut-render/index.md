Laser Cut Render Blender Add-on turns laser-cut SVG artwork into product-style 3D renders in Blender.

## What I Built

- Built an import workflow for 300 mm x 200 mm SVG files
- Interpreted red outlines as cut parts and blue outlines as vector engravings
- Treated grayscale artwork as raster engravings
- Used layer names as material hints for plywood, painted wood and acrylic
- Added a companion camera script for studio lighting and reusable render cameras

## Product Focus

The add-on helps bridge the gap between a flat laser-cut design and the polished preview needed for product listings, client sign-off or documentation.
