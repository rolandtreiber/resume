Lenstec approached me to build the internal order-management portal used by ASDA Opticians stores across the UK.

## What I Built

- Built the entire internal order-management system
- Delivered the project in three months using Laravel and Vue.js
- Created separate administrator and client-facing interfaces
- Added ordering, messaging, articles and announcements
- Delivered a system that remains in daily use by more than 300 ASDA stores nationwide

## Outcome

The portal became a core operational tool for ASDA Opticians order management and continues to support store workflows.
